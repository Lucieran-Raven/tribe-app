Privacy Policy — TRIBE

Last updated: September 11, 2026

TRIBE ("we", "our") is a social platform for students. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account info: name, email, and profile photo from Google Sign-In.
  • Profile info you provide: username (handle), bio, avatar, country, and affiliations (university, city, interests).
  • Content you create: posts ("rants"), replies, likes, and images you upload.
  • Notifications: a device push token (OneSignal) so you receive likes and replies.

2. How We Use Information

  • To operate your account and show your profile to other users.
  • To display your posts and replies in feeds and searches.
  • To send push notifications about activity on your content.
  • To moderate abuse (reports, blocks).

3. Where Data Is Stored

  • Account and content data: Google Firebase (Firestore), processed per Google's data processing terms.
  • Uploaded images: Cloudinary.
  • Push tokens: OneSignal.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data is shared only with the service providers listed above, strictly to operate the app.

5. Your Choices & Deletion

  • You can edit or remove profile info and content anytime in the app.
  • You can delete notifications in your inbox.
  • Account deletion: Settings → Delete Account removes your account, posts, replies, and notifications.

6. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Access to data is protected by Firebase security rules restricted to authenticated users and content owners.

7. Children

TRIBE is not directed to children under 13.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above reflects the latest version.
