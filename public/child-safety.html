TRIBE — Child Safety Standards

App: TRIBE  |  Developer: Lucieran & Angelina  |  Last updated: September 13, 2026

1. Our Commitment

TRIBE has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). TRIBE is intended solely for users aged 18 and older and is not directed to children.

2. Prohibited Content and Conduct

  • Any depiction, promotion, or solicitation of sexual abuse or exploitation of minors is strictly prohibited.
  • Any content that sexualizes, grooms, or endangers minors is prohibited.
  • Accounts operated by users under 18 are prohibited and removed.

3. How Users Report Child Safety Concerns

  • In-app: tap the menu (…) on any post, reply, or profile and select "Report", then choose the relevant reason.
  • By email: lucieranraven@gmail.com with the subject "Child Safety Report".

4. Our Response to Reports

  • Reports are reviewed promptly. Confirmed CSAM/CSAE content is removed immediately and the account is permanently banned.
  • We preserve evidence and report confirmed CSAM to the relevant regional and national authorities, including the NCMEC CyberTipline where applicable.
  • We cooperate with lawful law-enforcement requests.

5. Prevention Measures

  • TRIBE has no private direct messaging; interactions happen in public feeds and reply threads, reducing grooming risk.
  • Users can block others; blocked users cannot view or interact with the blocker's content.
  • Uploaded images are stored via Cloudinary and are removable on report.

6. Designated Contact

Child safety point of contact: lucieranraven@gmail.com
