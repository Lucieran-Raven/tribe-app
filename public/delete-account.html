TRIBE — Account & Data Deletion

App: TRIBE  |  Developer: Lucieran & Angelina  |  Last updated: September 12, 2026

How to delete your account and data

  1. Open the TRIBE app and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete Account.
  3. Confirm the deletion in the dialog that appears.

Alternative: email lucieranraven@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request" from the Google account you used to sign in. Requests are processed within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account record: name, email, username (handle), bio, avatar, country, and affiliations.
  • All your posts ("rants") and their uploaded images.
  • All your replies and your likes/votes.
  • All notifications in your inbox and your push subscription token.
  • Your blocked-users list.

What may be retained

  • Abuse reports involving your content may be kept for moderation and safety purposes.
  • Deletions apply immediately to live systems; backup copies are purged within 30 days.

Deleting individual data without deleting your account

Inside the app you can delete your own posts, replies, and inbox notifications at any time without deleting your account.
