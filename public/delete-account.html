TRIBE — Account & Data Deletion

App: TRIBE  |  Developer: Lucieran & Angelina  |  Last updated: September 13, 2026

How to delete your account and data

  1. Open the TRIBE app and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete Account.
  3. Confirm the deletion in the dialog that appears.

Alternative: email lucieranraven@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request". Requests are processed within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account record, posts, replies, likes, and notifications.

Deleting individual data

Inside the app you can delete your own posts, replies, and inbox notifications at any time without deleting your account.
